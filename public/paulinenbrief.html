PAULINENBRIEF

Anliegen an die Paulinen

MRT Forschungsanfrage — Paulinenkrankenhaus

Hallo liebes Paulinenkrankenhaus,

leider war die Untersuchung meiner Daten nicht mit den Erfolg gekrönt wie ich ihn mir erhofft hätte. Nach genauerer Recherche habe ich festgestellt, das ich lieber eine 3D-T1 Standardsequenz untersuchen wollen würde. Nach dieser hatte ich leider nicht explizit gefragt. Aber um mal Klartext zu reden meine eigentlich Intention. Wäre eigentlich mal mein merkwürdiges Brain selbstständig zu untersuchen. Ich leide schon lange an etwas was sich schwer in das klassische mentale Krankheitspektrum klassifizieren lässt. Um also mein Kopf genauer unter die Lupe zu nehmen. Bin ich grade auf der Suche nach der Möglichkeit. Selbstständige Forschung an meiner Gehirnstruktur zu betreiben. Ich weiß sie sind keine Universitätsmedizin aber ich denke doch wenn man wirklich Interesse am Fortschritt hat das auch dies keine Hürde darstellen sollte.

Und zur kapitalistischen Note: mein sozialer Nutzen ist fraglich. Ob sie mich nun Forschen lassen oder nicht. Ich kann sie nicht nur weil sie die rationale Alternative mir verwehren Ihnen weniger auf der Tasche liegen. Deswegen gehen sie dieses Minimalrisiko mich Forschen zu lassen doch einfach ein. Die meisten Leute finden die Kraft einen, aus meiner Sicht, chronisch lügenden Staatsapperats Blind zu vertrauen. Ich finde sie nicht.

35.000 bis 80.000 Nutzungszyklen und Eine Stunde Betrieb eines MRT-Geräts (Magnetresonanztomografen). Ein neues 1,5-Tesla-MRT-Gerät kostet netto zwischen 600.000 und 3.500.000 Euro. Meist werden günstigere Modelle gekauft aber als Benefit of the doubt: 2 Millionen / 57.000 = 35 €

Hinzu kommen kosten bei einer durchschnittlichen Anschlussleistung von 25 bis 30 kW und einem Strompreis von ca. 40 Cent pro kWh etwa 10,00 bis 12,00 Euro reine Energiekosten. Bei intensiven Scan-Sequenzen oder starker externer Klimatisierung kann der Wert auf bis zu 32,00 Euro pro Stunde steigen. (10+32)(€)/2 = 21 €

Ja ich weiß dazu können noch spezielle Gerätschaften, wie Magenfeldsichere Bildschirme und Elektronik. usw. dazu kommen. Diese sind aber Verhältnisweise Kleinigkeiten auch wenn sie aufgrund menschlicher Nutzung leichter mal einen defekt erleiden. 15.000€/10.000 Nutzungszyklen = 1,50€ +2,50 € Desinfektionsmittel und anderer Kleinkram. Hier muss man aber auch Sagen das selbst diese Dinger auch etwas Spielerei sind. Sie könnten genauso gut Glasfaser verwenden um das Signal an einen entfernteren Ort zu bringen. So macht man es ja beispielsweise mit Ultraschallgeräten.

Die Arbeit des Fachpersonals und für die Hände durch die das ganze zu wandern hätte, würde ich 30€/h berechnen. Ich finde das Großzügig berechnet, weil sie doch wohl auch einsehen das es sich bei diesen hierbei doch hauptsächlich um Notfallbereitschaft handelt. Hier möchte ich betonen bin ich gänzlich unkompliziert. Wenn irgendein Notfall reinkommen sollte, würde ich sofort abbrechen und wäre in einer Minute weg. Terminwahl ist ja auch deutlich flexibel! Auch in Liberia und abgeschiedenen Urwäldern gibt es heutzutage bereits MRT Maschinen.

Für die Miete eines 20 m², warm ~ 500€/mnth → 16,70€/d → ich finde wenn wir höher gehen sollten mit der Zahlung für die Fachkräfte, dann wäre es auch richtig mit dem Mietanteil runterzugehen, weil dieses prinzipell mehr Patienten pro Tag bedeuten würde. Eine Stundenabrechnung macht hier aber natürlich auch keinen Sinn. Deswegen: 1/3 × 16,70€/d = 5,56 €

Dann wäre mein großzügig gerechneter Vorschlag an Sie zu einer Stunde MRT zu Forschungzwecken:

(5,56 + 4 + 30 + 21 + 35) € = 95,56 €/h

bei einer 3D-T1 des Kopfes dann also 47,78 €

Gesamtkosten für 30 min 3D-T1 des Kopfes: 47,78 €

Hierbei wäre dann also keinerlei ärztliche Interaktion erforderlich. Ich würde mir jedoch freigeben wollen, wo und wie ich die Daten veröffentlichen möchte.

Ich freue mich auf Ihre Antwort und Hoffe auf eine baldige Rückmeldung

David Aßfalg, Patient

Vorbemerkung

Es ist mal wieder genauso eingetreten wie ich es prophezeit habe. Die Charité verweigert mir jegliche Form der Kommunikation. Ist es vorgreifende Diskriminierung oder nachgreifende IT-Überforderung? Man weiß es nicht! Hier bleibt das Ärztehaus so mysteriös wie Gottes Wege. Ich will hier nicht gleich die Nazi-Keule schwingen. CHARITÉ I

Open Letter to Medical Research

Stomach Cannula Concept & Open Questions

Hello Ladies and Gentlemen,

I wanna be part of an interesting experiment.

You guys may have heard of "Pansenfisteln" (rumen fistula cannula) in the context of cattle. Having a hole in the stomach seems to be a relatively easy operation endeavor. Which would allow an access to stomach fluid and gastric mucosa for permanent monitoring. It would also allow to filter out and replace food after consumption ensuring an optimized healthy intake for a diet while at the same time make calorie restriction and/or nutritional optimization possible. I imagine nothing more but a 2 cm hole for entrance. To make this happen with a ventile system 3 major questions need to be answered.

1) Comfort of wearing. It would be quite a problem if it wouldn't be able to feel good between the stomach wall. Therefore we use a silicone layer around the center piece.

2) Finding a material that can restrain all of these destructive enzymes and acidity. Is this already the end of the project? Well I feel polypropylene and PTFE (Teflon) might be the solution again. Although a thick "cold plasma sprayed" (or something) teflon surface over a silicone gel body might not stick well enough together. There are a few other solutions I got in mind. In theory it is possible to produce two or more overlapping net structures completely made out of teflon fibers — a consistent net structure that is also woven (or dissolved) into the silicone gel with advanced ceramics or titanium alloys presecuring further damage from impact and guaranteeing stability.

3) Accessibility. To make accessibility easy and safe a few magnets and a screwing head in place which activates a mechanical iris completely made out of teflon. If there is no pipe in place an additional placeholder is screwed over the relatively delicate mechanical iris structure also reaching over the skin, not touching the iris therefore no damage should be in place.

I think a system like this could be possible. If you think that is a preschool project which isn't worth even considering with all the other medical priorities in the back — you are probably right. Research should be based on solutions for everyone. Stem cell replacement, nanotechnology, biotechnology and even for interesting side projects for stupid interns stuff like nerve cuffs might be more productive. I got a few questions though...

Is overweightness not really a serious problem? Why is 80% of casual medical news and small talk medical topics based on eating advices? The idea that deficiencies cause a lot of body problems and that eating certain superfoods or just calorie restriction will highly increase your lifespan, and energy level is information you get everywhere so it does maybe help a little bit... although time consuming, abusable, risky and quite a lot of work.

But okay let's talk about doing something useful then. Do you guys got access to good small construction devices like this? No seriously, things like this or maybe magnetical methods and working with superconductors for 3D assembly in space are probably the key technologies for making small stuff happen. Yeah protein crystal growing and biohacking aside. Which is the key for everything from bioengineering to making cyborg brains. So thing is: I think I might be able to make this gel from the video. Having a pretty clear method in mind. I could use 1 or 2 pretty useful ingredients from Thermo Fisher though. An online store which needs a company name to deliver. Which would be nice to get. Then I'll be able to construct small and you got a nice device for making it happen. I mean maybe machines are actually considered dangerous because building small things could mean so many opportunities.

I dunno where you live but I can tell you where I live. I live in fcking hell. In the last years I went from feeling as the everyday dummie to fcking Einstein himself. Although I don't seem to able to grasp any of the basic concepts the world seems to run on and unlocking weird fears like being afraid that doctors just might kill a few million people by following a stupid DIN law or something. In this world I dunno when or what technologies will and can be used for our personal health wellbeing. Mostly because most medical institutions are so hidden and doesn't seem to care of removing the psychopathic monstrosity vibes they produce by simply being so incredibly weird, hidden and incredibly overinvested of hiding what their focuses actually are. Like especially state institutions. Like wtf is wrong with patent law in this country.

Although I would love to tell people how to make the gel sharing and all the other creative ideas. I just get the feeling a lot of essential and important stuff doesn't seem to be shared by you people or you invest all of your power to make it as hard as possible. And although it would make sense to not share any money with others for safety or maybe even the ridiculous apparatus to hide diseases distribution genetests and other important data. Cause of bad focus on the right disease focus. It is questionable for other things and although I don't get the subdivision in this nonsense. Doctors of Medicine maybe (and yeah only MAYBE) should have the right of a representation of the payment of the biggest insurance payers and the right to hide medical research data about the percentages of diseases occurring and their genetic links. But what about KEY Projects! Like projects where everyone is interested in and we can involve way more people close to everyone?

I'm talking about ways of iPS replacement and nanotechnological apoptosis and all the other ways we could imagine to achieve the three main goals of living forever with efficient brain and happiness. I might mention here: I don't believe in climate change. So the argument that we can just control everyone 24/7 might not occur completely. There is an insane safety network though. And if stuff like ways to produce magnets and this magic gel (no it's not trivial, no it's not water based, no you can't just google it or ask AI for the formula) is already secret because small construction can already be dangerous or something — the argument that chemistry to a certain point only needs electronic experts, neurologists, computer scientists and PCB designers might be true, so the machine alone knows and does the dangerous stuff with much less volume. But why are we doing the partly pretty dangerous chemistry with our hands then???? Why you hate us so much and should you not already consider giving me a laboratory for AI surveillance (or at least chemical expert surveillance, although I REALLY, REALLY don't get why I should do everything like old history and don't use modern powers), if I am already so close to producing this gel and there even are principle rules of "DmostHalal" (= only for men of culture) that should allow me limitless company access.

Cause you guys simply didn't tell us if we would be able to do things at all and I dunno in which way... man if this isn't the right address tell me which one is: I tried ministries, banks, hospitals, insurances aso. I wanna be a part of medical development in concrete projects and I don't wanna die of old age or suffer random old age diseases.

Can you please at least tell me if I'm blacklisted or anything? Like I'm on the still potential good people blacklist not the spammer list right, so you at least could tell me if I'm on it.

Sincerely,
David
